Trestle Run Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · Developer: [DEVELOPER NAME] · Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

Trestle Run doesn't collect, transmit or share any personal or sensitive information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and doesn't use the internet. Your progress stays on your device.

This policy covers the Trestle Run game for Android (package com.pre_release.Trestlerun), published on Google Play by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us").

What the game stores, and where

The game keeps your progress in its own private storage on your device: trails cleared, best times and medals, coins, items unlocked in the garage, achievements, daily and weekly challenge progress, your settings, and "ghost" replays of your best runs. None of it leaves your device and we never receive it.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game doesn't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and doesn't send any data over the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Permissions

The game asks for one Android permission, Vibrate, to give haptic feedback during play. It uses no other device features that involve your data.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads, installs and updates under Google's Privacy Policy. If you've allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may give us crash and performance reports (for example, the device model, Android version and where in the game a crash happened). These don't identify you, and we use them only to fix problems in the game.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Because the game sends no data anywhere, nothing is transmitted that could be intercepted. Your saved progress sits in the app's private storage, which Android keeps from other apps.

Keeping and deleting data

We hold no data about you, so there's nothing for us to keep or delete. To delete the progress on your device, uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Trestle Run → Storage & cache → Clear storage.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that uses data (for example, signing in to Google Play Games for achievements, in-app purchases, or ads), we'll update this policy and the game's Google Play data safety section before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
